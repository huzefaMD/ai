# Lesson 4
### 🦾 Without CoT vs With CoT

#### ❌ Without Chain of Thought

```text
Should I price my AWS course at ₹4,999 or ₹9,999?
```

**AI Response:**

> "I'd recommend ₹4,999 as it's more accessible for students."

**Problem:** Shallow answer. No reasoning. Could be wrong. 😐

---

#### ✅ With Structured Reasoning

```text
Should I price my AWS course at ₹4,999 or ₹9,999?

Analyze this step by step:

1. First analyze the target audience
2. Then consider competitor pricing in India
3. Then evaluate perceived value vs price
4. Then consider HuzefAI's current brand stage
5. Finally give your recommendation with reasoning
```

**AI Response:**

```text
Step 1 — Target Audience:
Your students are likely IT professionals and freshers in Chennai earning ₹3–8 LPA.
For them ₹4,999 is comfortable, while ₹9,999 requires stronger justification.

Step 2 — Competitor Pricing:
Udemy courses often sell for ₹455–₹799 during discounts. Local bootcamps typically charge ₹15,000–₹40,000.
 This places you in a strong middle position.

Step 3 — Perceived Value:
₹9,999 can signal higher quality and commitment.
Lower pricing may attract more inquiries but can reduce perceived value.

Step 4 — Brand Stage:
If HuzefAI is still building testimonials and case studies,
student acquisition may be more important than maximizing margins.

Step 5 — Recommendation:
Launch at ₹4,999 for the first batch to build social proof and collect testimonials.
 Increase pricing in future batches once results and success stories are established.
```

🔥 **The difference:** Instead of asking for just an answer, ask the AI to evaluate the factors that drive the decision.
