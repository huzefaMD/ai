# Lesson 4

## Without CoT vs With CoT

### Without Chain of Thought:

Should I price my AWS course at ₹4,999 or ₹9,999?

Claude's response: `I'd recommend ₹4,999 as it's more accessible for students`

Shallow. No reasoning. Could be wrong.

## With Chain of Thought:

Should I price my AWS course at ₹4,999 or ₹9,999?

Think through this step by step:

1. First analyze the target audience
   
3. Then consider competitor pricing in India
   
5. Then evaluate perceived value vs price
   
7. Then consider HuzefAI's current brand stage
   
9. Finally give your recommendation with easoning

## Claude's response: "Let me think through this carefully ...

*Step 1:*

Target Audience: Your students are likely IT professionals and freshers in Chennai
earning ₹3-8 LPA. For them ₹4,999 is comfortable, ₹9,999 requires justification.

*Step 2:*

Competitor Pricing: Udemy courses sell for ₹455-₹799 (discounted). Local
bootcamps charge ₹15,000-₹40,000. You sit in the middle - which is actually a strong
position.

*Step 3:*

Perceived Value: ₹9,999 actually signals HIGHER quality to serious students.
Cheap prices attract bargain hunters who don't complete courses.

*Step 4:*

Brand Stage: HuzefAI is growing - you need case studies and testimonials more
than margin right now.

*Step 5-*

Recommendation: Launch at ₹4,999 to fill your first batch fast, collect testimonials,
then raise to ₹9,999 for batch 2. Best of both worlds."

That's a real business decision, reasoned properly!

| Phrase | Use When |
|---------|---------|
| "Think step by step" | Any complex problem |
| "Walk me through your reasoning" | When you want to see the logic |
| "Break this down" | Complex topics or decisions |
| "First analyze X, then Y, then Z" | When you control the thinking order |
| "Think out loud" | When you want full transparency |
| "Before answering, consider..." | When you want Claude to weigh factors |
| "What are the pros and cons first?" | Before a recommendation |

## 3 Levels of Chain of Thought

### Level 1-Simple CoT
*Just add "think step by step" to any prompt:*

Should I run paid ads for HuzefAI right now?
Think step by step before answering.

### Level 2-Guided CoT

You define the thinking steps:

Should I run paid ads for HuzefAI right now?

Think through this in this order:

1. What's our current organic reach?
2. What's a realistic cost per lead in EdTech?
3. What conversion rate do we need to break even?
4. What's the risk if it doesn't work?
5. Give your final recommendation.

### Level 3-Socratic CoT

*Ask Claude to challenge its own thinking:*

Should I run paid ads for HuzefAI right now?

## Real Use Cases for HuzefAI

### Business Decision

I'm thinking of launching a new Generative AI
course at HuzefAI priced at ₹12,999.

Before recommending yes or no, think step by step:
1. `Analyze current market demand for Gen AI in India`
2. `Identify who would pay ₹12,999 for this`
3. `Consider what's already available (Coursera, Udemy)`
4.` Evaluate what makes HuzefAI's version unique`
5. `Assess the effort to build vs revenue potential`
6.` Give final recommendation with action steps`

### Curriculum Design

I want to build a 6-week DevOps course at HuzefAI
for complete beginners.

Think step by step:
1.` What foundational concepts must come first?`

2. `What's the logical skill progression week by week?`
   
3. `Where do students typically struggle most?`
   
4. `What hands-on projects reinforce each concept?`
   
5. `How should week 6 tie everything together?`

Design the full 6-week curriculum.

Think step by step. After your recommendation,
argue the opposite view. Then give your
final balanced conclusion.

Level 3 is incredibly powerful for important decisions - it forces Claude to see
both sides before concluding.


### Problem Solving

HuzefAI has 200 LinkedIn followers but our
posts get very low engagement.

Think step by step:
1.` Diagnose possible reasons for low engagement`

2.` Analyze what content works in EdTech on LinkedIn`

3.` Identify quick wins vs long term fixes`

4.` Prioritize top 3 actions I should take this week`

5. `Give me a 30-day engagement growth plan`

 Remember Roles from Lesson 2? Combine them with CoT for maximum power:

I want to grow HuzefAI from 50 to 500 students
