# Lesson 1

## PART 1: Writing with Claude

### The 3 Writing Modes

MODE 1: Claude writes FROM SCRATCH
You provide context + instructions
Claude produces the full draft

MODE 2: Claude writes FROM YOUR OUTLINE
You provide structure
Claude fills in the content

MODE 3: Claude writes IN YOUR VOICE
You provide examples of your writing
Claude matches your exact style

## Mode 1- Writing From Scratch
### Best for: Content you don't have time to start
```
<role>
You are an expert EdTech content writer
specializing in Cloud and AI courses.
</role>

<context>
HuzefAI is a Chennai-based training startup
teaching Cloud, DevOps, and AI.
</context>

<task>
Write a compelling About Us page for
HuzefAI's website.
</task>

<tone>
<tone>
Warm, founder-led, inspiring - like Huzefa
personally wrote it. Not corporate.
</tone>

<format>
3 short paragraphs:
1. Who we are and why we exist
2. What makes us different
3. Our promise to students
</format>

<length>
150 words maximum
</length>

<dont>
- No corporate buzzwords
- Don't start with "Welcome to HuzefAI"
- No passive voice
</dont>
```

## Mode 2- Writing From Your Outline
### Best for: When you know the structure but need help filling it
```
<task>
Expand this outline into a full
LinkedIn article for HuzefAI:

OUTLINE:
1. The problem: IT professionals fear
AI will replace them
2. The reality: AI replaces skills,
not people who have AI skills
3. The 3 AI skills every IT professional
needs in 2026
4. How to learn them fast
5. Call to action: HuzefAI course
</task>

<tone>
Authoritative but approachable -
like a knowledgeable friend
</tone>

<length>600-800 words</length>
```

## PART 2: Editing with Claude

Claude is an exceptional editor - better than most humans at catching issues.

### The 5 Types of Editing Claude Does Best

### Type 1-Clarity Editing

"Edit this for clarity.
Simplify complex sentences.
Make it understandable for someone
with no technical background.
Keep the meaning exactly the same."

[paste your text]

### Type 2-Tone Editing

"Rewrite this in a warmer, more
personal tone. It currently sounds
too formal and corporate.
Keep all the facts the same."

[paste your text]

### Type 3-Conciseness Editing

"Cut this down by 40% without
losing any important information.
Every sentence must earn its place."

[paste your text]

### Type 4- Power Editing

"Make this more persuasive and
compelling. Strengthen weak phrases.
Replace passive voice with active.
Make the opening hook stronger."

[paste your text]

```
<task>
Review this piece of writing and give me:
1. Overall assessment (2 sentences)
2. Top 3 strengths
3. Top 3 weaknesses
4. Specific suggestions for improvement
5. Rewrite the weakest paragraph
</task>

<text>
[paste your writing here]
</text>
```
