## PART 1: Understanding Templates & SOPs

### What's the Difference?

### TEMPLATE

`A reusable document
Fill in the blanks
Output focused`

*Example:* Email draft

*WHEN TO USE TEMPLATE:*

`Writing something
Creating content
Communicating`

### SOP

`A step-by-step process
Follow the steps
Process focused`

*Example:* How to onboard
a new student

*WHEN TO USE SOP:*

`Doing something
Following a process
Operating a system`
