# Lesson 8

## The Ultimate Prompt Template

Here is the master template that combines every technique:

```
<role>
Who Claude should be - specific expert identity
with years of experience and domain focus
</role>

<context>
Background information - who you are,
your company, current situation, goals
</context>

<audience>
Who this output is for - their background,
knowledge level, what they care about
</audience>

<task>
Exactly what you want Claude to produce -
clear, specific, unambiguous
</task>

<thinking>
Think step by step:
1. First consider X
2. Then analyze Y
3. Then evaluate Z
4. Finally conclude with W
</thinking>

<examples>
Example 1: [Show what good output looks like]
Example 2: [Another example if needed]
</examples>

<do>
- Positive instruction 1
- Positive instruction 2
- Positive instruction 3
</do>


<dont>
- Never do this
- Avoid this
- Don't ever do this
</dont>

<format>
Exact structure of the output you want
</format>

<tone>
The precise voice, style and personality
</tone>

<length>
Specific word or paragraph count
</length>
```

You don't need ALL sections every time - use what's relevant. But for high-stakes,
important outputs - use them all.

## Example for HuzefAI

```
<role>
You are a senior EdTech marketing strategist 
with 10 years experience launching online 
courses in India. You've helped 30+ EdTech 
startups create successful course launches 
that sell out within 48 hours.
</role>

<context>
HuzefAI is a Chennai-based online training 
startup founded by Huzefa. We teach Cloud, 
DevOps, and AI to IT professionals and 
freshers. We currently have 50 students and 
are launching our first Generative AI course 
priced at ₹9,999. Launch date is next Monday.
We have: 200 LinkedIn followers, WhatsApp 
list of 150 leads, and 50 past students.
</context>

<audience>
IT professionals aged 22-35 in Chennai and 
across India. They are ambitious, career-focused, 
worried about AI replacing their jobs, and 
actively looking to upskill. They respond to 
peer success stories and fear of missing out.
</audience>

<task>
Create a complete 7-day launch campaign 
for HuzefAI's Generative AI course that 
maximizes enrollments before Monday launch.
</task>

<thinking>
Think step by step:
1. Analyze the audience's biggest fear and 
   desire around AI right now
2. Identify the best message angle for 
   this specific audience
3. Plan content for each of the 7 days 
   leading to launch
4. Design channel specific content for 
   LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Email
5. Build urgency sequence for final 48 hours
</thinking>

<examples>
Strong hook example:
"Your company just hired an AI tool that does 
your job in 10 minutes. What's your plan?"

Strong CTA example:
"30 seats. Monday launch. Reply YES to hold yours."
</examples>

<do>
- Lead with emotion — fear of being left behind
- Use real student success language
- Make Huzefa's founder voice come through
- Create genuine urgency — not fake scarcity
- Personalize for Chennai IT market
</do>

<dont>
- Don't sound like a generic course advertisement
- Never use "Excited to announce"
- Avoid corporate buzzwords
- Don't make unverifiable claims
- Never use more than 2 hashtags per post
- Don't make it all about features — focus on outcomes
</dont>

<format>
Day by day breakdown:
- Day (Monday-Sunday)
- Theme for the day
- LinkedIn post (ready to publish)
- WhatsApp message (ready to send)
- Key goal for that day
</format>

<tone>
Authentic founder voice — passionate, 
direct, slightly urgent but never pushy. 
Sounds like Huzefa personally talking 
to each person, not a brand broadcasting.
</tone>

<length>
Each LinkedIn post: 150-200 words
Each WhatsApp message: 60-80 words
Full campaign fits in one document
</length>
```
