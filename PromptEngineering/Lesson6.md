# Lesson 6

## PART 1: Controlling TONE
**Tone is the personality and emotion behind the words.**

## The Tone Spectrum

|FORMAL| CASUAL|
|------|-------|
|Corporate|Friendly|
|Professional|Conversational|
|Academic|Playful|
|Authoritative|Warm|

## Tone Reference Guide


| Tone | Use Case | Example Phrase |
|---|---|---|
| Professional | Business emails, reports | `"We are pleased to inform you..." `|
| Conversational | Social media, blogs | `"Here's the thing nobody tells you..." `|
| Authoritative | Thought leadership | `"The data is clear on this..." `|
| Empathetic | Customer support | `"I completely understand how frustrating..." `|
| Exciting/Energetic | Launch announcements | `"This changes everything. Here's why..." `|
| Humorous | Casual content | `"Spoiler: it's not what you think " `|
| Urgent | Limited offers | `"Seats closing in 48 hours..." `|
| Inspirational | Motivational content | `"One year ago, he couldn't spell AWS..."` |
